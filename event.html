The Woolly Worms Are Here.

Activity Guide

Kids' Arts and Crafts

Paint, color, and make paper-chain woolly worms! Add your name to the Visitor's Mural which will be auctioned Sunday night. All proceeds from the sale will go to the Margret Anchor Young Artists Foundation.

Live Entertainment

Local musicians perform throughout Saturday and Sunday. Classic hometowm hits like the Preston Benfield Band will be back. All music is pre-approved and family friendly!

Woolly Worm Races

"Tiny but mighty" is our motto at the 31st Annual Woolly Worm Festival! Enter a fuzzy friend and be the next Grand Champion! Watch woolly worms compete in heats during all open festival hours.
